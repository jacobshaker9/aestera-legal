Aestera — Terms of Use and Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Aestera is a daily routine app that starts with posture. One short routine a day, about 8 minutes, plus a 30-second stand-tall reset. Check it off, keep your streak, and track progress with on-device photos. No scores, no face ratings, no accounts.

The daily routine, checklist, streak, reminders, and photo capture are free. Aestera Plus is an optional upgrade that adds photo comparison, progression, and analytics (see Section 3).

Privacy Policy

The short version

Aestera collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone. Free and Plus users are treated the same: neither creates an account or sends data anywhere.

1. Information we collect

None. Aestera does not collect, transmit, store on a server, sell, or share any personal information. There is no account system, so there is nothing to sign up for and no profile to build.

2. Information stored on your device

The following stay on your device only and are never sent anywhere:

  • Your checklist and streak progress.
  • Your settings, such as your reminder time.
  • Your progress photos, if you choose to take them.

Other app data follows your device's normal backup settings. Your progress photos are excluded from device backup, so they do not transfer to a new device and are lost if the device is lost or replaced. Restoring a purchase restores Plus access, not photos.

3. Permissions

Aestera asks for only two permissions, and only when you use the related feature:

  • Notifications: for your local daily reminder only. Reminders are scheduled on your device. Nothing about them leaves the device.
  • Photos and camera: only if you choose to add progress photos. Photos are stored on your device only, are never uploaded, and are never used for any rating or analysis.

You can decline either permission and still use the rest of the app. You can change permissions later in your device Settings.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, see, or store your payment details.

5. Third parties

Aestera uses no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting software. The only third-party framework involved is Apple's own StoreKit, which handles purchases on-device through the App Store.

6. Children

Aestera collects no information from anyone, including children. The app is intended for users aged 13 and older. If you are under 13, do not use the app.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete your photos and all app data in the app's settings at any time. Deleting the app also removes its on-device data. Because we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing to request from us.
